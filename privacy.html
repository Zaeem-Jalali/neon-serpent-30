Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Plain-language summary: Neon Serpent 30 can be played with no account at all — in that mode your scores and settings never leave your device. If you choose to sign in (Google or as an anonymous guest) so your progress and leaderboard scores sync across devices, we store the minimum needed to make that work: an account ID, an optional display name, and the runs/levels you complete. We do not sell data, run ads, or track you across other websites.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers Neon Serpent 30 (the "Game"), a browser-based game published by Zaeem Jalali, together with its optional account and leaderboard features. It explains what the Game collects from you, why, and what control you have over it.

The Game is an independently developed project, not a service operated by a company. "We" and "us" below refer to the developer, Zaeem Jalali.

2. Information we collect

a. Stored only on your device (always, regardless of account status). Game progress, unlocked levels, best scores, your chosen palette and skin, sound preference, and player name are stored in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you sign in and it is synced, as described below. It is not a cookie and is not used to track you across other sites.

b. Account information (only if you sign in). Neon Serpent 30 offers two sign-in methods:

  • Anonymous guest sign-in creates a random account identifier and nothing else. No personal information is collected.
  • Google sign-in receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google, used only to identify your account. Your email address is never displayed publicly or shown to other players. If you set a leaderboard display name, that name — and only that name — is what other players see.

c. Gameplay data. If signed in: your completed runs (score, level reached, game mode, and, for Daily Rift, the shared daily seed), and your campaign progress (which levels are unlocked/completed). This powers the leaderboard and lets progress follow you across devices.

d. Automatically collected technical data. Our hosting provider(s) may log standard web-server data (IP address, browser type, request timestamps) for security and abuse prevention, in line with their own privacy policies. We do not separately aggregate or analyze this data ourselves.

We do not collect payment information, precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access, or browsing activity outside this site.

3. How we use information

  • To let you play, save progress, and pick up where you left off across devices.
  • To operate the public and Daily Rift leaderboards.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (e.g. rate limiting, fraud prevention).
  • To respond if you contact us.

We do not use your information for advertising, do not build behavioral profiles, and do not sell or rent personal information to anyone, under any circumstances.

4. Legal bases for processing (EEA / UK / Switzerland)

Where GDPR or equivalent UK/Swiss law applies, our bases for processing are: your consent (choosing to sign in), performance of a contract (providing the game/leaderboard service you requested), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and functioning correctly. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (Section 8).

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The Game itself sets no advertising or analytics cookies. If you sign in, our authentication provider stores a session token in your browser (via localStorage or a cookie, depending on the sign-in method) purely so you stay signed in between visits. This is strictly necessary for the sign-in feature to function and is not used for tracking.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell your data. Limited data is shared only with the service providers (sub-processors) that make the Game work, each bound by their own privacy and data protection terms:

  • Supabase (database and authentication) — stores account and gameplay data described in Section 2.
  • Google (Sign in with Google) — provides basic profile information if you choose that sign-in method.
  • Our hosting provider (e.g. Netlify) — serves the site's files and may log standard technical request data.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of players or the Game itself.

7. Third-party links

The Game may link to third-party sites (for example, a donation page such as Ko-fi or Buy Me a Coffee). Those sites have their own, separate privacy policies; we are not responsible for their content or practices, and encourage you to review their policies before using them.

8. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored data persists until you clear your browser's site data. Account data is retained for as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the details in Section 13 — we will act on verified requests within a reasonable time and in any event within the timeframe required by applicable law.

9. Your privacy rights

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland (GDPR/UK GDPR): you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data, and to object to certain processing. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

If you are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Other jurisdictions: we aim to honor equivalent access, correction, and deletion requests for all players regardless of location, on a best-efforts basis.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13.

10. Children's privacy

Neon Serpent 30 is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction, if higher), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age through the account/sign-in system. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it. The no-account, local-only mode of the Game collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

11. Data security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including industry-standard access controls (Row Level Security) restricting account data so that only the account it belongs to can read or write it. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. International data transfers

The Game is operated from Pakistan. Our service providers (see Section 6) may process and store data in countries other than your own, including the United States and the European Union, relying on those providers' own safeguards for cross-border transfers. By using the account features, you understand your data may be processed outside your country of residence.

This policy is written in plain language and is intended as a complete and honest description of how the Game handles your data, informed by the GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA/CPRA. It is a statement of our practices, not legal advice to you. If any part of it is unclear, or you believe something here does not match how the Game actually behaves, please contact us using Section 13 — we would rather correct it than leave it ambiguous.

13. Contact us

Questions, requests, or concerns about this policy or your data: jalalizaeem@gmail.com. We aim to respond to privacy requests within 30 days.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game changes. Material changes will update the "Last updated" date above; continued use of the Game after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
